Proudly Independent – Globally Focused

Today, AccuSpan is one of the most dynamic, agile, and fastest-growing independent suppliers of calibration gases in the world. Our team boasts a wealth of experience from individuals who have developed and led the business over the past 40 years.

At AccuSpan, we specialise in providing high-quality calibration gases, Specialty gases, and advanced gas solutions for various industries. Our offerings include span gas, bump gas, and ISO 17025-certified gases to meet your analytical and testing needs. In addition, AccuSpan can offer a range of regulators and other equipment, providing a one-stop shop for all your global calibration gas needs, all at very competitive prices.
