NRC Equipment

AccuSpan offer the following equipment to be used for NRC products. For more information please contact us directly.

FF100 - Fixed Flow Regulator

  • Available in Ni-Brass or Stainless Steel
  • C10 connection which fits the 34DA, 60DA,112DA and 103DS cylinders
  • Includes an on/off valve with cylinder pressure gauge

ODFR – On-Demand Flow Reg

  • This regulator is for use with instruments that utilise a pump to draw calibration gas or sample
  • C10 connection which fits the 34DA, 60DA,112DA and 103DS cylinders
  • Can be used for reactive and non-reactive mixtures
  • Available in Ni-Brass and Stainless Steel with C10 connection

Variflow Regulator

  • The Variflow Regulator can be set to a number of fixed flow settings between 0.5 LPM to 8.0LPM flow rate.
  • C10 connection which fits the 34DA, 60DA,112DA and 103DS cylinders
  • For use with reactive and non-reactive gases
  • Available in Ni-Brass and Stainless Steel with C10 connection

Aerosol Flow Controller

  • Fits the 12DA non-refillable cylinder which is fitted with a screw 7/16” thread aerosol valve.
  • Includes an on/off valve with a mini flow meter for accurate setting

All regulators provided with a 24 month warranty. Many in stock which and can often be shipped next working day.

NRC Accessories

AccuSpan offer the following accessories to be used for NRC products. For more information please contact us directly.

ACC001 Carry Case

  • The ACC001 soft carry case with its shoulder strap makes it easy to transport up to 3 cylinders and equipment safely whilst keeping both hands free. Very useful when climbing up ladders and working on gangways.

Tygon Tubing

  • AccuSpan only supplies Tygon tubing and it is available in a range of lengths. The Tygon tubing can be used for all gases and functions and isbetter than PVC to reduce gas absorption due to the inner Teflon coating for reactive gases.

HPC Equipment

AccuSpan offer the following accessories to be used for HPC products. For more information please contact us directly.

Single Stage Brass Regulators

  • Primarily used for non-reactive gas mixture applications up to 200 bar
  • Single-stage for low pressure applications
  • Standard ¼” NPT (F) port regulators can be supplied with connectors to fit BS, DIN and CGA cylinders.

Two Stage Brass Regulators

  • Primarily used for non-reactive gas mixture applications up to 200 bar
  • Two-stage regulator for applications requiring a consistent outlet pressure and flow rate
  • Standard ¼” NPT (F) port regulators can be supplied with connectors to fit BS, DIN and CGA cylinders.

Single Stage Stainless Steel regulators

  • Primarily used for reactive gas mixture applications up to 200 bar
  • Single-stage for low pressure applications
  • Standard ¼” NPT (F) port regulators can be supplied with connectors to fit BS, DIN and CGA cylinders.

Two Stage Stainless Steel regulators

  • Primarily used for reactive gas mixture applications up to 200 bar
  • Two-stage regulator for applications requiring a consistent outlet pressure and flow rate
  • Standard ¼” NPT (F) port regulators can be supplied with connectors to fit BS, DIN and CGA cylinders.

FF100 Fixed Flow Reg
