Uniphos Detector Tubes

Uniphos detector tubes offer easy chemical measurement in gas samples. Pre-calibrated and maintenance-free, they come in over 250 options for more than 150 chemicals and concentration ranges.

Gas List

The ASP-40 precision piston hand pump is recommended for use with Uniphos indicator tubes; however, they can also be used with GasTester and Kwik Draw hand and electric pumps. The indicator tubes we offer are characterised by long durability, high quality confirmed by manufacturer's tests, and a wide measurement range for each of the measured substances.

Due to their properties, indicator tubes are used in various settings. Most often in industrial plants, especially in the chemical industry, but also in sewage treatment plants, power plants, metalworks, and petrochemical plants. They are frequently used by fire brigades and rescue teams at sites of fires, accidents, or chemical leaks.

Uniphos indicator tubes enable the measurement of gas concentrations in the atmosphere in a very simple and reliable way. They do not require calibration by the user and are characterised by high measurement accuracy.

Please contact us if you want to know more details.
