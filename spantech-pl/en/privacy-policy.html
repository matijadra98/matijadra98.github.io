Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Spantech Sp. z o.o. processes the personal data of visitors to www.spantech.pl and of people who contact us, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is Spantech Sp. z o.o., Al. Krakowska 1, 05-552 Wola Mrokowska, entered in the register of entrepreneurs of the National Court Register kept by the District Court for the Capital City of Warsaw, XIV Commercial Division of the National Court Register, KRS 0000622244, NIP 123 131 94 98, REGON 364 680 850.

In all matters relating to the processing of personal data, you can contact us by email at info@spantech.pl or by phone at +48 22 487 98 44.

2. What data we process

Depending on how you use our website and contact us, we may process the following data:

  • Data provided through the contact form or by email: name and surname, email address, phone number, company name, the subject and content of the message.
  • Data related to the use of the website: IP address, information contained in server logs, browser type and version, and other technical data needed to ensure that the website works correctly and securely.

3. Purposes and legal basis of processing

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Handling enquiries and taking steps before entering into a contract – on the basis of Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, where these steps are taken at the request of the data subject.
  • Conducting ongoing business correspondence and handling contacts with company representatives – on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, i.e. our legitimate interest in maintaining business relationships and handling enquiries.
  • Performing contracts and customer service – on the basis of Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Meeting legal obligations, in particular those arising from tax and accounting regulations – on the basis of Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.
  • Ensuring the correct operation and security of the website, including detecting errors, preventing unauthorised access and keeping server logs – on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Establishing, pursuing or defending against possible claims – on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

4. Recipients of data and transfers outside the European Economic Area

Your personal data may be shared with or entrusted to external entities where this is necessary to run our business and achieve the purposes listed above.

Recipients of data may include in particular:

  • providers of IT, hosting and website services, including Odoo S.A., based in Belgium;
  • providers of email services and IT tools, including Microsoft;
  • courier and transport companies, to the extent necessary to make deliveries;
  • accounting firms and providers of bookkeeping services;
  • Spantech Products Ltd (United Kingdom), a company of the Spantech group, where the transfer of data is necessary to handle an enquiry, for business cooperation or to fulfil an order.

Personal data is transferred to the United Kingdom on the basis of a European Commission decision confirming an adequate level of personal data protection.

Where we use providers in connection with whom personal data may be transferred to the United States, such transfers take place using the appropriate legal mechanisms provided for in the GDPR, in particular the European Commission decision on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, where the recipient holds the relevant certification, or standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission, together with the required safeguards.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your personal data for the period necessary to achieve the purpose for which it was collected:

  • data related to enquiries and business correspondence – for the time needed to handle the enquiry and complete the correspondence, and then for the period needed to secure possible claims;
  • data related to the performance of contracts – for the duration of the contract, and then for the time required by law;
  • accounting and tax records – for the period required by applicable law;
  • technical data and server logs – for the period needed to ensure the security and correct operation of the website.

After the relevant periods, the data is deleted or anonymised, unless further storage is required by applicable law.

6. Your rights

In connection with the processing of personal data, you have the following rights, on the terms and to the extent set out in the GDPR:

  • the right of access to your personal data;
  • the right to rectification of data;
  • the right to erasure of data;
  • the right to restriction of processing;
  • the right to data portability;
  • the right to object to processing based on the controller's legitimate interest;
  • the right to withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it was withdrawn.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority, which in Poland is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa).

Providing personal data is voluntary, but it may be necessary to answer an enquiry, prepare an offer or conclude and perform a contract. Not providing the required data may make it impossible to achieve these purposes.

Your personal data is not subject to automated decision-making, including profiling, that would produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you.

7. Cookies

Cookies are small text files saved by your web browser on your device.

Our website uses only technical cookies necessary for it to work correctly, in particular to keep your session, ensure security and remember your chosen language.

We do not use cookies for advertising or to track user activity for profiling or behavioural advertising.

You can change your cookie settings in your web browser at any time. Please note, however, that disabling necessary cookies may limit or prevent the use of some features of the website.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the event of changes in the law, technological changes or changes in the way the Controller processes personal data.

The current version of the Privacy Policy is always available on our website.
