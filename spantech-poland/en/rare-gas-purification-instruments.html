Rare Gas Purification Instruments

We supply Sircal's Heated Getter Gas Purifiers for rare gases such as argon, helium, neon, xenon, and krypton.
Sircal’s rare gas purification instruments are renowned globally for their reliability and technical excellence.

Contact us

Established in 1981, Sircal Instruments (UK) Ltd specialises in rare gas purification systems, particularly for metal analysis using the Arc/Spark spectrometry technique. With over 30 years of experience, they design, manufacture, and distribute rare gas purifiers worldwide.

Acquired by Fire Testing Technology Limited (FTT) in 2010 and a subsidiary of Judges Scientific plc, Sircal benefits from extensive R&D, manufacturing, and after-sales support.

Based in East Grinstead, UK, they are known for their rigorous quality standards and competitive pricing, making them the world leader in point-of-use argon gas purification systems.

Rare Gas Purifier Model MP-2000

Introduced in the year 2000 and now further improved with a new microcontroller circuit with LCD display and optional pressure sensor (from March 2009). The MP2000 is designed to deliver purified argon, helium or other rare gas at the point of use.

Learn more

PD-2012 Rare Gas Dryer

The PD-2012 Rare Gas Dryer is a portable standalone unit for drying argon or any rare gas: helium, neon, xenon and krypton, in laboratories or on-site in-line between your gas supply and instrument, e.g. Arc/Spark Optical Emission Spectrometers.
