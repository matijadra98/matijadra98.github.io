About us

Spantech Sp. z o.o. began operations in Poland in 2016, building on experience dating back to 1977 when Dr Chandramani Narain founded Spantech Products Ltd in South Godstone, United Kingdom.

We distribute calibration gases, gas generators, gas sensors, and equipment that enhances gas purity.

Our clients span various industries, including environmental protection, metallurgy, mining, chemistry, automotive, food, medicine, and science. Focusing on tailored solutions, we ensure constructive collaboration with clients, understanding their needs and striving to build long-term relationships.

Choose Spantech.pl for professional service and optimal solutions that guarantee customer satisfaction.

Spantech Products Ltd

The Spantech family business began in 1977 as Spantech International and remains family owned and run to this day. Spantech Products, formed in 1983, has specialised in calibration gas since the mid-1990s and is now a global network — but the way we work hasn't changed: you speak to someone who understands the application before you buy.
