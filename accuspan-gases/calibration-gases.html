What Are Calibration Gases?

Calibration gases are specially formulated gas mixtures used to test and calibrate gas detection equipment, analysers, and other gas-measuring instruments. These gases are essential for ensuring equipment functions accurately, reducing the risk of false readings and non-compliance.

Our calibration gases are:

  • Available in a range of cylinder sizes, including portable non-refillable cylinders (NRCs) and high-pressure refillable cylinders (HPCs).
  • Designed for industrial and environmental applications.
  • Produced to ensure consistent quality and accuracy.

Our Calibration Gas Product Range

We offer a diverse selection of calibration gases designed for various applications. Below are some of our most popular products:

Single-Gas Calibration Cylinders

Ideal for calibrating gas detectors that measure one specific gas type. Available options include:

  • Hydrogen Sulphide (H₂S): Essential for detecting toxic gases in the oil and gas industry.
  • Sulphur Dioxide (SO₂): Commonly used in environmental monitoring and industrial safety.
  • Ammonia (NH₃): Ideal for gas detection in industrial and environmental settings.
  • Chlorine (Cl₂): Critical for calibration in water treatment and safety applications.

Multi-Gas Mixtures

Designed for multi-gas detectors used in industrial and environmental monitoring. Our quad and quint mixtures include combinations like:

  • H₂S, SO₂, CH₄, CO, CO₂, O₂ in N₂: Tailored for versatile testing needs across multiple applications.

Non-Reactive Gases

We supply a range of non-reactive gases, including:

  • Nitrogen (N₂)
  • Methane (CH₄)
  • Propane (C₃H₈)
  • Helium (He)
  • Hydrogen (H₂)
  • Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)

These gases are ideal for calibration in industrial and laboratory environments.

Specialty Gases

Our specialty gases are designed for precise calibration in specialised applications:

  • Ethylene Oxide (ETO): For medical equipment, fruit ripening, and sterilisation calibration.
  • Ethanol (C₂H₅OH): Widely used in breath testing, manufacturing, and research calibration.
  • Hydrogen Chloride (HCl): Applied in chemical manufacturing safety calibration.
  • Hydrogen Cyanide (HCN): Vital for mining and industrial gas detection systems.
  • Nitric Oxide (NO) and Nitrogen Dioxide (NO₂): Common in air quality and environmental monitoring.

Cylinder Sizes

At AccuSpan, we offer a comprehensive range of Non-Refillable Cylinders (NRCs) and High-Pressure Refillable Cylinders (HPCs) to suit your operational needs:

  • 112-Litre NRCs (112DA): Ideal for applications requiring a larger gas volume, providing an extended supply for comprehensive calibration tasks.
  • 60-Litre NRCs (60DA): Suitable for applications requiring moderate gas volumes.
  • 34-Litre NRCs (34DA): Offers a balance between portability and capacity, ideal for regular calibration procedures.
  • 12-Litre NRCs (12DA): Ultra-portable and low-cost, designed for quick and convenient calibrations in confined or remote locations.
  • 375-Litre NRCs (375DA): NEW! Our highest-volume non-refillable cylinder, designed for workshop or remote locations where transport costs are high.

In addition, we provide High-Pressure Refillable Cylinders (HPCs), which are an excellent choice for applications that demand larger gas volumes and longer usage periods. These durable, cost-effective, and reusable cylinders are ideal for facilities with frequent calibration needs.

Applications of Calibration Gases

Calibration gases are widely used across many industries, including:

  • Industrial Safety: Gas detectors in factories, refineries, and chemical plants rely on proper calibration to protect workers from toxic or explosive gases.
  • Marine: Gas detection on vessels using both NRCs and HPCs for fixed and portable gas detectors. These gases are increasingly used for stack emissions, especially for NOx and SOx.
  • Oil & Gas: Supports compliance with safety requirements for gas detection in offshore rigs, petrochemical facilities, pipelines, refineries, and mud logging applications.
  • Environmental Monitoring: Essential for accurate readings in air quality monitoring stations and environmental assessments.
  • Research and Development: Ensures precise gas measurements in laboratories and testing facilities.
