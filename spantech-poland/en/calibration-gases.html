Calibration Gases

Mixtures to order · flow regulators

For over 25 years, Spantech has been supplying high-quality calibration gases to clients worldwide. We specialise in creating mixtures tailored to the individual needs of our customers. Our offerings also include high-quality flow regulators that ensure precise dosing of these mixtures.

Spantech is a global supplier of high-quality specialty gases, headquartered in Stoke, United Kingdom.

Our mission is to deliver calibration gas mixtures made with the highest possible precision and to provide our clients with comprehensive support in calibrating devices for gas detection and concentration measurement. Our goal is to become the preferred choice for industries, including the analytical and manufacturing sectors.

To prevent contamination and the effects of atmospheric humidity, all our gas mixtures are produced in controlled environmental conditions. Before the mixing process, the purity of the gases we use is confirmed through preliminary analysis. Our equipment is continually calibrated, allowing us to maintain high measurement accuracy and, as a result, deliver a product of exceptional quality.

Our gas mixtures are supplied with a composition certificate, which includes detailed information on all mixture components, concentration accuracy, pressure, gas volume, storage information, and shelf life.
