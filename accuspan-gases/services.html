Our Services

Delivering Excellence in Calibration and Specialty Gases

At AccuSpan, our services are built on a foundation of precision, quality, and safety. We are committed to providing reliable calibration and specialty gas solutions, ensuring smooth logistics, rigorous quality assurance, and industry-leading safety standards.

  • Logistics
  • Quality Assurance
  • Safety Standards
Contact our team

Logistics

Efficient and dependable logistics are at the heart of our operations. We ensure timely delivery of calibration gases, specialty gases, and equipment worldwide, with solutions tailored to meet your schedule and specifications.

Dangerous Goods Shipping

Many of our products such as NRC and HPC gas mixtures are classified as IATA Dangerous Goods. It is important that these are shipped correctly with the right paperwork. AccuSpan can arrange all this for you with our trained DG staff. AccuSpan staff are fully IATA trained in handling and shipping Dangerous Goods. We ship to just about every corner of the world.

Express Delivery UK

We also operate our own transportation and can deliver throughout the UK using our own transport if this is needed. AccuSpan can provide a range of certification including Certificates of Origin provided by the local Chambers of Commerce.

Lead Time

AccuSpan has one of the shortest lead times in the business. Many products are held in stock at our UK manufacturing plant and can be shipped next day. For non-standard mixtures, we offer fast manufacturing and shipping times. Whilst mainly using the main Logistics carriers, AccuSpan uniquely has its own transport enabling emergency shipments to be made to UK destinations when time is critical.

Private labelling

AccuSpan can provide private labelling for customers at no extra cost. This enables you to brand the product specific to your own business.

One Stop Shop

AccuSpan can provide a range of complex gas mixtures in both NRC cylinders and High Pressure Cylinders. Often AccuSpan can offer mixtures that other companies will not supply due to internal restrictions.

Returns, Collection and Delivery

AccuSpan can arrange returns or collection of empty cylinders. AccuSpan can offer nationwide delivery and collection of HPC cylinders with our ADR trained drivers.

Quality Assurance

Quality is non-negotiable at AccuSpan. All our products meet the strictest international standards, including ISO 17025, ISO 17034, and ISO 11118 certifications. From gas mixtures to cylinder manufacturing, our processes are designed to deliver consistent and reliable results, ensuring you receive the best products every time.

  • AccuSpan is ISO 9001:2015 accredited.
  • All AccuSpan gas mixtures are manufactured using scales calibrated with NPL weights in the UK.

Safety Standards

AccuSpan puts safety first in everything we do both with regards to manufacturing, shipping and providing advice to our customers on handling and storing gas cylinders.

  • We will provide a Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for all gas mixtures supplied electronically.
  • The SDS number is shown on the label of each cylinder supplied so that the customer can easily access the information saving time and keeping everyone safe!
  • AccuSpan can supply SDS’s in a range of European languages including, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese and many others.

Proudly Independent – Globally Focused

AccuSpan is proudly independent. We offer a fast agile service in terms of quoting, lead times and shipping. You can always talk to a person at AccuSpan and not a system. We really do value your business.

Pricing

AccuSpan prides itself on supplying the highest quality gases with the most competitive pricing in the marketplace. As an Independent company we can offer outstanding value for money to meet your calibration gas needs with industry leading customer service and lead times.
