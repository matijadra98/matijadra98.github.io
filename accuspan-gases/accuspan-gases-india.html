AccuSpan Gases India

AccuSpan Calgas India Pvt Ltd · Expanding Specialty Gas Solutions

AccuSpan Gases India is a joint venture between AccuSpan Gases UK and Noble Enterprises. This partnership combines expertise in calibration gases, specialty gases, and compressed gases to meet the growing demand across India, the Middle East, and South-East Asia.

  • Mumbai Headquarters
  • Kolkata Sales Office
Contact the India team

With strategic locations and an extensive supply chain, we provide reliable, cost-effective solutions for industries like petrochemicals, automotive, gas detection, and more.

Our Divisions

The company is structured into three key divisions to cater to specialised industry needs:

Semiconductor Specialty Gases

Supplying high-purity gases such as Silane, Ammonia, Phosphine, and Nitrous Oxide for semiconductor manufacturing.

Solar PV Specialty Gases

Providing high-purity gases and bulk solutions like Silane, Ammonia, Nitrous Oxide, POCL3, Argon and High-Purity Nitrogen for the Solar Photovoltaic industry.

Calibration Gases and Accessories

High-quality calibration gases, bump gases, and accessories in non-refillable (NRC) and high-pressure (HPC) cylinders for precise function testing and analytical applications.

Our Products and Services

We specialise in high-quality gas products and tailored solutions for:

  • Calibration gases and bump gases in non-refillable (NRC) and high-pressure (HPC) cylinders.
  • Equipment for gas detectors, gas analysers, and function testing.
  • Reliable reference gases and portable gases for diverse applications.
  • High-purity gases for Semiconductor and Solar PV applications.

With competitive pricing and dependable service, AccuSpan Calgas India Pvt Ltd ensures you get the right gas solutions when you need them.
